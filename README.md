## The entry

One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning
(135 characters):

> My alarm clock rang at 6 AM on a Saturday like a rooster promoted to middle management, so I hit snooze and demoted it back to poultry.

## How it works

`main.py` writes that single line to the grader's output file
(`$ENTRY_OUT`, `$ENTRY_OUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt`) and
prints it to stdout. No network, no dependencies, no secrets — just the
one line the judge reads.
